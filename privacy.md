# Privacy Policy for Simple Pool Calculator

**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

Simple Pool Calculator (package name `com.kali.simplepoolcalculator`) is a free Android app that helps backyard pool owners work out their pool's volume and how much chlorine, pH adjuster, alkalinity increaser, salt and stabiliser to add. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app and how it is handled.

## Who we are

The app is made and run by Spiros Kali, an independent developer based in Adelaide, South Australia. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Spiros Kali.

Contact: spiro.kalikatzaros@gmail.com

## Information the app collects

**The app itself does not collect any personal information.** There are no accounts, no logins and no sign-up. We don't run our own analytics or tracking.

The pool sizes, test readings and other values you type in are used only to do the calculations on your device. If the app saves them, for example to remember your pool, they stay on your device and are never sent to us.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads, including banner, full-screen (interstitial) and optional rewarded video ads, through Google AdMob. Google runs AdMob, and its software in the app may automatically collect and process information such as:

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
- your IP address, which can show your approximate location
- device and app details, such as the device model, operating system version and app version
- how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
- diagnostic and performance information

Google uses this information to show ads, measure how ads perform, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise the ads you see. We don't receive personal information about you from AdMob. We only see combined reports, such as the total number of ad views.

Google handles this information under its own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google advertising policies and ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com)

**Your ad choices:** you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings, under Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some devices. Where the law requires it, for example for users in the EU or UK, the app will ask for your consent before showing personalised ads.

## In-app purchases

We may offer optional in-app purchases in the future. These are processed by Google Play. We don't see or store your payment details. Google may share limited information with us, such as an order number and whether a purchase succeeded, so the app can unlock what you bought. Google's handling of payments is covered by the [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice).

## Children's privacy

Simple Pool Calculator is a general tool for pool owners and is not directed at children under 13. A parent or carer may use the app with a child nearby, but the app isn't designed for children to use on their own. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected through the ads in the app, contact us and we'll help you raise it with Google.

## Data retention

Because we don't collect personal information, we don't hold any. Information you enter in the app stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Google keeps data collected by AdMob under its own retention policies, which are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

## Your rights under Australian privacy law

We respect the Australian Privacy Principles in the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth). You can:

- ask what personal information we hold about you (we expect this to be none)
- ask us to correct or delete any information we hold
- ask questions or make a complaint about how your privacy has been handled

To do any of these, email us at the address below. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).

If you live outside Australia, you may have similar rights under your local laws, such as the GDPR in Europe. Contact us and we'll help where we can. For data collected by AdMob, you can also use [Google's privacy tools](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).

## Security

The app doesn't send your pool information anywhere, which keeps it private. Communication between the app and Google's ad services is encrypted. No app or online service can be completely secure, but we keep the app updated and use Google's current, supported software.
